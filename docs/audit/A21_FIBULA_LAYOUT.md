## Finding

In the anatomical position the fibula is the **lateral** leg bone, slightly posterior to the tibia. The canonical
data had `FIBULA_L/R` on the tibia's own knee → ankle segment (z 0.487 → 0.0702), at the same x and 0.0333 m
**in front** of it (y −0.0333). This was found in A20 (finding 1), and it is the same defect as the forearm before
A11.

## F2 re-run (tibia + fibula vs A20)

Per-bone metrics are **identical** to A20: tibia L/R RMS 3.68 / 2.99 mm, length +12.1 / +12.6 %; fibula L/R RMS
0.22 / 0.05 mm, length +8.2 / +8.6 %, scale 1.136 / 1.140. A per-bone similarity fit cannot see a pure
translation of the bone. A20 had suggested that the offset inflated the fibula scale and length; that was wrong,
and A20 is corrected.

Where the fix shows is the tibia → fibula relationship. In BodyParts3D (one subject, 1.72 m, internal use: it
is evidence, **not** a source) the fibula's slice centroid lies 20–32 mm lateral and 18–36 mm posterior to the
tibia's (medians ≈ 23 mm lateral, ≈ 26 mm posterior, both sides). Canonical before: 0 lateral, 33 mm anterior,
so ≈ 63 mm from that relationship. After: 33 mm lateral, 0 antero-posterior, ≈ 28 mm. The posterior part stays
pending a source.
